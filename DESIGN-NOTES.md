# Design Direction

LEETECH & AUTOS is a technology and automotive brand, not a generic gadget catalogue or an automotive-only dealer. The redesign uses one consistent editorial language across both collections: warm charcoal, ivory, a deep red signature, generous spacing, real photography, and restrained movement.

## Reference Research

- **GetLayers**: cinematic depth, deliberate first-viewport composition, slow photographic entrances, and a recognizable brand signal. The site uses original implementation, not copied premium templates or generated product scenes. Reference: https://www.getlayers.ai/
- **Land-book**: automotive and ecommerce examples informed the photographic hierarchy, distinctive product layouts, large editorial headings, and quieter footer treatment. The public Autohaus reference was studied for category hierarchy and image-led browsing, without copying its branding or layout. References: https://land-book.com/?industry=Automotive and https://land-book.com/design/ecommerce
- **Grainient**: atmospheric color depth is expressed using subtle CSS gradients, most visibly in the newsletter area. No AI-generated background assets are used. Reference: https://grainient.supply/
- **Mobbin**: public purchasing and product-detail flow descriptions informed straightforward discovery, filtering, product information, cart adjustments, and checkout validation. Shopping remains familiar and accessible rather than becoming an animation demo. Reference: https://mobbin.com/explore/web/flows/purchasing-ordering
- **Reelfolio**: slow camera-like image reveals, clean section entrances, and restrained hover movements inform the pacing. No showreel video or third-party design assets are copied. Reference: https://reelfolio.io/

## Typography

Space Grotesk gives the LEETECH wordmark scale and authority. Instrument Serif adds a classic editorial contrast to AUTOS and selected headings. Manrope keeps product names, prices, navigation, and forms crisp and practical. Fonts load from Google Fonts and their SIL Open Font License files are included in `public/fonts/`.

## Photography Policy

Product and promotional imagery comes mainly from official manufacturer sources: Apple, Samsung, Sony, ASUS, Mercedes-Benz, Land Rover and Lexus. Four items are exceptions and need replacing with official photos when available: the Mercedes-AMG GLE 53 (2025) image (CarBuzz), and the MacBook Air M2, Galaxy Buds3 Pro, and the Apple 20W and Samsung 25W chargers (retailer listings). No Pexels or other stock photography is used. Product images are stored as transparent WebP cut-outs in `src/assets/products/` so each product sits directly on the storefront surface with no photo background. The hero uses the same cut-out approach. No images are AI-generated by LEETECH. Source pages for every cut-out are listed in `src/assets/products/SOURCES.md`, and the on-site photography credits dialog has been removed.

The Latest Devices section keeps its original official Apple and Samsung images, loaded from the manufacturers' own sites. Manufacturer imagery remains owned by its manufacturer; review its permissions before commercial publication. CSS shading improves text readability without creating or changing depicted objects.

## Motion And Accessibility

The initial photograph settles into place, sections reveal once as they enter view, and product photographs move gently on hover. A very subdued CSS gradient shifts behind the newsletter section. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Dialogs retain keyboard focus management, Escape dismissal, native form validation, and mobile layouts down to 320px.

## Latest Devices

The latest-device showcase sits immediately below the hero and trust row, before general shopping categories. Its default view is iPhone 18 Pro Max, not an older best seller. Four accessible tabs switch between verified 2026 Apple and Samsung models in a single photographic composition. Device details show manufacturer-confirmed specifications and available configurations. Selecting a finish and configuration prepares a real WhatsApp enquiry; it does not place an order or claim stock. Apple describes MacBook Pro M6 as announced with availability from November 6, 2026, which is stated explicitly.

Official sources: https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/, https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-s26-ultra/, and https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/.
